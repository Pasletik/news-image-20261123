naming the day, two lead stories, three columns of short story blocks
across world politics, economy, science, sport, disasters and culture,
a day-in-UTC timeline strip and a date-check footer. Thirty-seven
stories, each verified against at least two sources dated 23 November
2026 (see `sources.md`).

## How it works

`render.py` draws `entry.png` (1024x1024) with Pillow: fixed layout,
fixed text, deterministic output. `main.py` (Python standard library
only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`) and
exits 0.
